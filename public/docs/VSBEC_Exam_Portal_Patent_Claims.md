# PATENT SPECIFICATION DOCUMENT: CLAIMS

## TITLE OF THE INVENTION
**AN AUTONOMOUS EXAMINATION MANAGEMENT, ANONYMIZED DUMMY NUMBER MAPPING, AND ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE-DRIVEN EVALUATION SCHEME GENERATION FRAMEWORK**

---

### WE CLAIM:

1. **An autonomous examination management and evaluation system (AEE-DMSGF) comprising:**
   - an **Optical Barcode & Dummy Number Anonymous Mapping Engine** configured to ingest student examination registration numbers, validate attendance records, and generate non-reversible cryptographic pseudorandom dummy identifiers for answer scripts to facilitate blind valuation;
   - an **Artificial Intelligence-Powered Question Paper Analysis & Solution Rubric Generator** coupled to a Large Language Model (LLM) processor, configured to extract structural question hierarchies, categorize questions according to Revised Bloom's Taxonomy cognitive levels, map questions to Course Outcomes (COs), and generate step-by-step model answer keys and rubric score distributions;
   - an **Autonomous Role-Based Continuous Assessment Lock/Unlock Controller** configured to enforce strict temporal mark-entry windows for faculty across academic years, semesters, and sections, preventing post-deadline modification;
   - a **Multi-Tier Mark Computation & Autonomous Grading Engine** configured to ingest internal assessment marks, practical marks, and end-semester scores, automatically computing weighted aggregates, 10-point Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) letter grades, SGPA, and CGPA; and
   - a **DLT-Compliant Real-Time Institutional Telemetry & Alert Dispatcher** configured to automatically trigger SMS and messaging notifications for attendance shortages, Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) marks, and end-semester results to registered guardian communication channels.

2. **The system as claimed in claim 1, wherein:**
   - said **Optical Barcode & Dummy Number Anonymous Mapping Engine** comprises a bidirectional cryptographic hash lookup table stored in an encrypted Controller of Examinations (COE) vault;
   - said engine replaces student roll numbers with dynamic pseudo-random alphanumeric barcodes on detachable answer sheet counterfoils prior to physical or digital distribution to evaluators; and
   - said engine includes an automated parity check module that identifies missing scripts, duplicate dummy allocations, or unmapped attendance mismatches prior to valuation dispatch.

3. **The system as claimed in claim 1, wherein:**
   - said **AI-Powered Question Paper Analysis & Solution Rubric Generator** parses uploaded question papers in digital format, performing optical character recognition and semantic tokenization;
   - said generator programmatically assigns cognitive domain levels ranging from Level 1 (Remembering) to Level 6 (Creating) pursuant to Bloom's Revised Taxonomy; and
   - said generator produces a standardized evaluation key comprising sub-question breakdown, partial credit rules, alternate solution pathways, and syllabus module tagging formatted directly into an exportable institutional template.

4. **The system as claimed in claim 1, wherein:**
   - said **Continuous Assessment Lock/Unlock Controller** provides granular academic administrative access controls categorized into Admin (COE), Head of Department (HOD), Subject Faculty, and Examination Squad roles;
   - said controller executes an automated programmatic freeze on mark submission inputs upon timestamp expiration of an examination schedule; and
   - said controller permits selective administrative unlocking with mandatory digital audit trail logging, requiring reason specification, authorization timestamps, and prior score version archiving.

5. **The system as claimed in claim 1, wherein:**
   - said **Multi-Tier Mark Computation & Autonomous Grading Engine** executes dynamic normalization across multiple question parts and internal test cycles (CIA-1, CIA-2, Model Examination);
   - said engine maps raw percentage scores into categorical 10-point grade thresholds comprising Outstanding (O), Excellent (A+), Very Good (A), Good (B+), Above Average (B), Pass (C), Re-appear (U), Shortage of Attendance (SA), and Withdrawal (W); and
   - said engine calculates Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) and Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) according to course credit weightage matrices with automated fail-safe rounding and transcript generation.

6. **The system as claimed in claim 1, wherein:**
   - the system further comprises a **Multimodal Discrepancy & Optical Verification Module** configured to scan valuation marks sheets and optical mark recognition (OMR) response grids;
   - said module cross-correlates digital mark entries submitted by evaluators against dummy barcode tallies; and
   - said module flags statistical anomalies, out-of-bound marks, unanswered question over-allocations, and totaling discrepancies before unmasking student identities.

7. **The system as claimed in claim 1, wherein:**
   - said **DLT-Compliant Institutional Telemetry & Alert Dispatcher** links directly to an automated SMS gateway pre-configured with regulatory Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT) template headers; and
   - said dispatcher dispatches personalized student academic progress metrics, test-specific mark breakdowns, class averages, and automated warning advisories for cumulative attendance falling below the seventy-five percent (75%) statutory threshold.

8. **A computer-implemented method for autonomous examination administration, comprising the steps of:**
   - generating an anonymized dummy identifier for an enrolled candidate associated with a specific subject code, semester, and academic year;
   - automatically parsing an examination question paper through an LLM engine to derive a structured answer key, question-level Bloom's taxonomy tags, and partial-marking scoring rubrics;
   - receiving evaluation score vectors mapped to said anonymized dummy identifier within an administratively monitored time-lock window;
   - validating valuation entries through automated range checks and anomaly detection filters;
   - computing final normalized grade points, course attainment values, and GPA statistics; and
   - cryptographically de-anonymizing said dummy identifier to publish verified mark transcripts and dispatch automated DLT-compliant parent alerts.
